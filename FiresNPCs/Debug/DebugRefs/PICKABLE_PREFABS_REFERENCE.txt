=== NOTES ===

1. Berries respawn after 300-450 minutes (5-7.5 hours)
2. Mushrooms/herbs respawn after 240 minutes (4 hours)
3. Crops and ore pickables do NOT respawn (respawnTime = 0)
4. Wild crops (Barley_Wild, Flax_Wild) also don't respawn

5. Companions should probably:
   - Always forage berries, mushrooms, herbs
   - Pick up ground resources (flint, stone, branches)
   - Maybe NOT auto-harvest player crops (configurable?)
   - Pick ore when near water/swamp for tin/iron
