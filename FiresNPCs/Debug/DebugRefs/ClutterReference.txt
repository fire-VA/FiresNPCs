=== IN-GAME CONTROLS ===

E on Dirt Floor piece:
  - Toggles between grass/cultivated state

Shift + E on Dirt Floor piece:
  - Cycles through ALL available grass types
  - Works in any biome
  - Selection is saved and persists

=== CONSOLE COMMANDS ===

listclutter [grass|all]
  - Lists all clutter entries to BepInEx/config/FiresRPGmaker/clutter_dump.txt
  - Use 'grass' filter to only show grass entries

setgrassmat <clutter_name>
  - Sets the default grass material for new pieces
  - Example: setgrassmat instanced_heathgrass

=== WHY ONLY INSTANCED_ PREFABS? ===

Only instanced_ prefabs from ClutterSystem have properly configured:
- Materials with correct shaders (Custom/Grass)
- Textures that render correctly on billboards
- Alpha cutout for transparency

Non-instanced clutter (magicGrass, DeepNorthGrass, etc.) use different
shaders that don't display correctly on our billboard quads.
