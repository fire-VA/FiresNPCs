## 0.0.688
- The globe HUD can be dragged by its artwork and resized wide or tall.
- New Player > Leaderboard page in the F1 hub with the full leaderboard window, and Leaderboard | Achievements pills. The leaderboard key is now unbound by default.
- Cleaner settings screen: only the main hotkeys and toggles show, the rest is under Show Advanced (your saved values are kept).
- The NPC route map shows the real NPC walking live, with a trail.
- Live map floors: step through a building level by level, with roofs hidden.
- New optional live minimap (off by default).
- Territories: owners can let their party or guild in, and turn on raid and offline protection, which stops other players damaging, removing or using pieces, chests, doors, stands, portals and beds.
- Territory owners can edit access to their own territories (Public / Only me presets), and admins can limit territories per player.
- Fixed the Dressing room tabs not switching when clicked.
- Fixed party invites and territory ownership not syncing to other players in multiplayer.
- Fixed guild and party access to territories not working for other players.
- Fixed RevealOnMap territory flags with several values turning into a plain radius reveal.
- Fire started by a player now counts as that player for raid protection, so it can't burn down protected bases. Lightning, lava and monster fire are unchanged.

## 0.0.662
- New Dressing room for NPCs: Setup NPC with labelled fields, the live NPC with 10 gear slots (helmet, chest, legs, cape, utility, both hands, both backs, pet) and a Wardrobe to dress it from (Fires All The Items, or your inventory without it).
- Appearance changes show on the NPC at once; nothing is saved until Apply Changes. Animation previews play on the NPC itself.
- Model Override in Setup NPC has a dropdown of the allowed models.
- The NPC's anchor orb shows only from the admin menu or the new "Show anchor" toggle.
- The admin NPC book (Shift+E) opens in the Fires look every time.
- NPC patrol routes are walked and kept after a relog.
- Fixed the pause menu sometimes not opening after closing the NPC book.
- Live map zooms in to a single building, and stays sharp.
- Chat, quest journal, trader, NPC info and HUD boxes move and resize in the Fires layout mode; chat, quest tracker and territory box can be dragged at any time, and layout Reset puts them back.
- The territory box hides on the small map while a full-screen window is open.
- Territories take your character's size into account.
- Requires Fires Unified Core 0.2.580.

## 0.0.567
- Static NPCs no longer trigger an AI-guard warning after load.

## 0.0.566
- Non-combat pets (follow, emotes, item stands) and fixes.
- Updated for Valheim 1.0.
- Liquid globe HUD: health, stamina, eitr and adrenaline slosh as you move and show value popups and tooltips; it remembers where you put it and how big you made it.
- NPCs given another model (a creature, or the female player body) walk, emote and greet on that model, and town NPCs wear outfits from the 1.0 gear list.
- NPCs placed from a template belong to whoever placed them, so workers can use that player's private chests and work inside their wards.
- Your deaths and what killed you now count on the leaderboard and on memorial cards.
- A helmet hides a companion's or NPC's hair, and taking it off puts the hair back.
- The territory map overlay uses far less memory and stutters less on long sessions.
